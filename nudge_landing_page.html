why this feels calming

Most productivity apps are built like control panels.

Bright colors. Aggressive dashboards. Endless widgets. They demand attention instead of reducing mental load.

This is intentionally softer. Warm neutrals calm the nervous system. Rounded layouts reduce visual tension. Serif typography slows reading slightly, improving emotional comfort and retention.
